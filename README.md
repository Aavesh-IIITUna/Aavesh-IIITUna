## 📌 About Aavesh

> *Aavesh is the Electronics and Development Club of **IIITU**, driving a culture of hands-on learning, applied research, and open innovation.*

We work at the intersection of **Electronics, Embedded Systems, AI/ML, and Software Development** — and this repository is the living record of what our members go on to build, research, and contribute to, both inside and outside the club.

## ✍️ How to Add Your Name

<details>
<summary><b>Click to expand contribution steps</b></summary>
<br/>

We keep this README community-maintained. To add yourself to any table above:

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. Add your row to the relevant table, following the existing format.
3. Keep entries **one line, factual, and concise**.
4. Open a **Pull Request** titled `Add: <Your Name> - <Category>`.
5. A maintainer will review and merge it.

Not comfortable with Git? Fill out **[this form](#)** instead and a maintainer will add your entry.
